# Purpose

Provide a lightweight personal, self-hosted board that replaces paid Miro.
There are no user accounts, authentication, collaboration or required SaaS
backends in the accepted product scope.

Future functions follow migration of a representative board, especially a board
containing links, images, product references and notes. The replacement must
make migration losses visible rather than promise complete Miro compatibility.

See [vision](VISION.md), [architecture](ARCH.md) and [proposed roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
